# AI usage

AI accelerated the research workflow; it did not supply the evidence.

## Where AI helped

- identifying suitable official sources and likely point-in-time failure modes;
- scaffolding parsers, validation tests and documentation;
- proposing alternative chart layouts and concise recruiter-facing language;
- adversarial review for causality, unit, licensing and look-ahead claims; and
- checking that the final narrative distinguishes observations from interpretation; and
- identifying a rights-minimal publication boundary for upstream artifacts.

## What remained deterministic

- source downloads and SHA-256 hashes;
- field extraction and unit handling;
- sanitization of publication-calendar facts and the four-value price extract;
- managed-money net calculations;
- peak, trough, latest and percentage-change calculations;
- effective-time and available-time filtering;
- chart data and every displayed metric; and
- reproducibility and integrity tests.

Those operations are implemented in Python. The PDF renderer refuses to build if its expected
headline values do not agree with the derived outputs.

## Human controls

Official source pages and downloaded artifacts are the evidence of record. Numerical claims are
checked against deterministic outputs, and interpretive language is reviewed for overstatement.
No model-generated quotation, source, observation or estimate is accepted without verification.

Full World Bank workbook and CFTC page-wrapper bytes are not published. Official URLs and
upstream hashes preserve provenance, while deterministic extracts retain only the facts required
to reproduce this study.

AI output is never treated as a market-data source. This project does not ask a model to decide,
size, route or execute a trade, and it contains no broker connection or automated trading action.
