# AI usage

I used AI tools for source discovery, code, tests, charts and writing. Parallel assistants also
reviewed timing, units, licensing and interpretation. These were development reviews, not an
independent external audit.

Python and workbook formulas calculate positioning, prior-only thresholds, event responses,
confidence intervals, revisions, weather features and hypothetical option payoffs. Official
publications and attributed extracts supply the observations. AI output is not a market-data source.

Source URLs, retrieval times and hashes are retained. Tests check calculations, publication timing
and reported figures. Numerical results remain inconclusive where the evidence is weak, and the
trade stays unevaluated without licensed inputs. Scientific studies inform hypotheses rather than
supplying price forecasts.

The Word notes are editable summaries of the retained results. Source interpretation and changes
still require human review. No model places or routes trades.
